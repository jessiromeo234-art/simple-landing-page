Clear Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our circular materials, plastic-free delivery, and living soil biology.

Why is 100% peat-free soil crucial for the climate?
Peat bogs cover only 3% of the world's land surface but store nearly 30% of all land-based carbon. Harvesting peat releases massive amounts of ancient carbon into the atmosphere. Our living soil utilizes aged forest humus, buffered coconut coir, and biochar to deliver superior aeration without destroying peatlands.
How do subterranean irrigation ollas work?
Ollas are unglazed terracotta vessels buried neck-deep in your garden bed. When filled with water, moisture slowly leaches through the porous clay walls directly to surrounding plant roots based on soil suction, cutting surface evaporation by up to 70%.
Do the biodegradable coconut seed trays really break down?
Yes! Unlike plastic pots that end up in landfills or 'biodegradable' PLA bioplastics that require industrial heat, our coconut husk fiber trays decompose naturally in your backyard compost or soil bed within 8 to 12 weeks, adding valuable organic mulch to the earth.
How does your 100% plastic-free shipping work?
We use unbleached recycled cardboard boxes, water-activated kraft paper tape, and biodegradable cornstarch cushioning peanuts that dissolve instantly in warm water.
